# Research & Verification Notes

This file documents the evidence categories behind the portfolio concept without publishing the employer’s original test-assignment document.

## Product verification

Current product and pricing facts used in the implementation were re-checked against Linked Helper’s official pages in September 2026:

- Personalization Suite: https://www.linkedhelper.com/features/personalization-suite
- Linked Helper homepage: https://www.linkedhelper.com/
- Pricing: https://www.linkedhelper.com/pricing

These sources were used to re-check product availability, pricing, trial information, AI Draft review/approval, Custom Variables, IF–THEN–ELSE fallback behavior, reply-aware controls, support, and current product positioning.

## SEO research

The original assignment used a 15-query semantic core. Keyword discovery was checked with Ahrefs Free Keyword Generator for Google / United States and then filtered by SERP intent.

The selected primary keyword was:

`personalized LinkedIn outreach tool`

The key decision was to prioritize commercial / feature-page intent over raw search-volume ranking when the higher-volume variants produced mixed informational SERPs.

## Voice of Customer

The assignment used public review evidence from G2 and Trustpilot to identify recurring jobs, pains, and desired gains around:

- scaling outreach without losing a personal feel;
- reducing manual research and rewriting;
- maintaining campaign control;
- avoiding shallow personalization;
- handling setup complexity and support needs.

The testimonial excerpts used on the concept page originate from the approved Voice-of-Customer section of the completed assignment and are attributed to their review platforms on the page.

## Claim discipline

The concept intentionally avoids:

- invented reply-rate or conversion metrics;
- absolute safety claims such as “100% safe” or “zero risk”;
- treating AI personalization as unique by itself;
- treating CSV Custom Variables as unique by themselves;
- showing unsupported autonomous AI sending;
- representing IF–THEN–ELSE as arbitrary value comparison instead of presence-based fallback logic.

The aim is to demonstrate research-backed product marketing rather than inflate the concept with unsupported claims.
